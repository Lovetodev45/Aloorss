```
Hi everyone! I made ÆON, a browser incremental about guiding humanity through
9 ages — from the first spark of consciousness to cosmic Transcendence.

What's in it:
• 4 resources that feed each other (Knowledge is the currency; Energy & Culture
  boost it; Population boosts everything)
• 35 buildings, 30 discoveries, and Doctrines — mutually exclusive tech choices
  that change every run
• Two prestige layers: Transcendence (Sparks) → Cosmic Awakening (Singularities
  + permanent Decrees)
• Legacies & the Creator's Dilemma: each transcendence engraves a permanent trait
  AND quietly asks whether you're growing a civilization… or sacrificing it
• Challenges (no-click run, ascetic, speedrun…) with permanent rewards, plus a
  hidden secret one
• A real WebGL Julia fractal "map" of your civilization, shaped by your choices —
  zoom in infinitely
• Golden Sparks, tiered crits (Crit/Hard/Mega), missions, a news ticker for flavor
• A narrative with an identity twist and a secret ending
• 100% offline (PWA, installable), no ads, no tracking, nothing leaves your browser

Feedback very welcome — especially on pacing and clarity. Thanks for playing!

▶ https://lovetodev45.github.io/Aloorss/
```
